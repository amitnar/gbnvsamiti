Grassroots Interventions

Integrated Development In Action

We work across key social domains to deliver long-lasting, sustainable improvements in rural livelihoods and human dignity.

Education

Shiksha Vikas Abhiyan

Free supplementary education centers operating in 22 villages. We provide learning kits, remedial classes for board exams, and basic digital literacy for rural kids.

Key Milestone: 8,500+ children enrolled | 94% school retention rate.
Volunteer Now
Livelihoods

Swayamsiddha Mahila Kendra

Empowering rural women through vocational tailoring, sanitary napkin production units, and financial literacy workshops to foster micro-entrepreneurship.

Key Milestone: 3,200+ women trained | 18 Active Self Help Groups.
Volunteer Now
Healthcare

Aarogya Gram Seva

Conducting monthly mobile medical checkup camps, distributing free essential medicines, anemia screening for teenage girls, and maternal health awareness.

Key Milestone: 140+ Camps Conducted | 18,000+ Free Prescriptions.
Volunteer Now
Environment

Harihara Vasundhara Drive

Large-scale afforestation and tree plantation drives across Gautam Buddha Nagar to combat industrial emissions and preserve indigenous biodiversity.

Key Milestone: 15,000+ Native Saplings Planted & Maintained.
Volunteer Now
Digital Education

Gramin Computer Literacy

Equipping rural schools with solar-powered computer labs and tablet computers to introduce coding and basic IT skills to village youth.

Key Milestone: 12 School Solar Computer Labs Set Up.
Volunteer Now
Clean Water

Shuddha Jal Abhiyan

Installing community RO clean drinking water plants in fluorosis-affected villages in Jewar and Greater Noida rural belts.

Key Milestone: 8 Community Water Plants Installed.
Volunteer Now
