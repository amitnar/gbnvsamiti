Our Founding Journey

Driven By Grassroots Passion & Purpose

Gautam Buddha Nagar Vikas Samiti (GBNVS) is a non profit social organization registered under the Societies Registration Act. We are working with a collective developmental approach to uplift rural communities, empower women, and educate underprivileged children.

Founded in Gautam Buddha Nagar district, Uttar Pradesh, GBNVS started as a localized youth initiative addressing education gaps and health neglect in agrarian villages. Over the years, our work has evolved into a structured social development organization serving over 45 villages across Noida, Greater Noida, and Jewar region.

Our Vision

To create an inclusive, self-reliant, and ecologically harmonious society where every child receives quality education, every woman achieves financial dignity, and every rural household accesses healthcare and clean environment.

Our Mission

To execute community-centric developmental programs through active multi-stakeholder collaboration, sustainable skill building, grassroots healthcare delivery, and environmental conservation drives.

The Framework

The Collective Developmental Approach

GBNVS functions on a 4-dimensional ecosystem model that bridges policy, community, corporate CSR, and youth volunteers.

1. Community Ownership

Local Gram Panchayats and self-help group leaders co-design initiatives, ensuring local relevance and long-term sustainability.

2. Corporate CSR Synergies

Partnering with industrial and corporate enterprises in Noida/Greater Noida for impactful CSR allocation and compliance.

3. Youth & Student Volunteers

Engaging university students and young professionals in active weekend teaching, tree planting, and medical drives.
